MarkMello desktop application — Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 13, 2026

MarkMello is maintained by Andrey Ermolaev (dartdavros).
Project: https://github.com/dartdavros/MarkMello
Full policy and contact: https://markmello.ru/privacy

LOCAL DOCUMENTS
MarkMello opens, renders, edits and saves documents locally on your device.
It does not upload your Markdown documents to MarkMello servers for processing.
Reading preferences, application settings and the automatic update preference
are stored in your user's application configuration directory.

UPDATE CHECKS AND DOWNLOADS
Automatic update checks are enabled by default. With this option enabled,
MarkMello checks GitHub Releases 30 seconds after the first window opens.
You can disable this option on the installer's Additional Tasks page, or later
under Settings > Updates. Turning it off stops pending automatic checks.
Your choice is retained across upgrades. Manual checks remain available.
Downloading and installing an update are separate actions you initiate.

Update requests disclose ordinary connection information, including your IP
address and request headers, to GitHub and its delivery infrastructure. They
do not upload your document contents. GitHub's privacy statement:
https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

REMOTE IMAGES AND LINKS
Displaying an HTTP(S) image referenced in a Markdown document contacts that
image's host. The host can receive your IP address, the requested URL and
request headers. The document author chooses the image URL and its parameters.
Opening an external link contacts that website. Its own privacy policy applies.

DIAGNOSTICS AND WEBSITE ANALYTICS
The desktop application does not include product analytics or behavioral
telemetry by default. Local diagnostics support troubleshooting.
Yandex Metrica is used on markmello.ru only, subject to the website's analytics
choice; it is not included in this desktop application or installer.

QUESTIONS
Contact the maintainer through https://github.com/dartdavros.
Do not disclose private documents or credentials in public issues.
